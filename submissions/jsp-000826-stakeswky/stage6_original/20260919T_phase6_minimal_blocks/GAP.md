## Old remaining domain

All actual lex-minimum counterexample forests, with the inherited all-vertex, proper-induced-subforest and shared-edge-deletion constraints. Under the pinned component-30 result, at least one tree component has order >=31. Connectedness and uniqueness of that large component were not known.

## New excluded configurations and dependencies

A proper nonempty LC component block is legally removable while preserving non-unimodality: otherwise LC times unimodal would make the original forest unimodal. Thus every proper component block of a minimum counterexample is non-LC. This argument has a self-contained proof and no computational dependency.

Using the fixed external census and empty H3, the small part must be empty, a bank singleton, or one of the 97 H2 pair multisets. Three small components are impossible. The 247 count is only 1+149+97 small index classes. The full external census and H2/H3 were not rerun; three trees and two pairs were independently recounted as selected controls. The restriction is conditional, not a proof of an unconditional census bound.

## New remaining domain

A single non-LC tree of order >=31 is still possible. So is an arbitrary number of large components together with zero, one or two permitted small components, subject to all minimality constraints. Every nonempty proper mixed block must be non-LC as well. Neither large component count nor order, terminal arm lengths, true exterior size, or internal profiles has a proved finite bound.

A uniform actual-forest construction with arbitrarily many large components and all component blocks non-LC shows that this block condition alone cannot give such a bound. It is not a counterexample to ORIGINAL or to the complete minimum-counterexample premises.

## First unproved connection

Every non-path tree has a terminal-spider center. This applies to each remaining component. At such a center with chosen pendant arm length l and actual exterior, the exact identities are

    P=a_l E+b_l L,
    P_arm_deleted=E+L,
    P_cut=a_l(E+L),
    Q=P_cut-P=(a_l-b_l)L.

All original other components and exterior vertices remain in E,L. Given a true valley Delta P_i=-s<0<Delta P_j=t, showing

    Delta Q_i<s and Delta Q_j>-t

would retain the valley after this edge cut. A different valley after a legal reduction would also suffice, but must be proved from the new complete sequence. No theorem supplies such an operation for every remaining forest. The fact that the deleted/cut forest is unimodal is a premise of minimality, not an elimination.

Thus the chain fails at **mandatory structure -> legal elimination or contradiction**. The finite diagnostics and exact formulas do not reduce the earlier universal real-forest residual incompatibility. J_H and RSM remain unresolved alternatives; neither is assumed true or refuted here.
