# The changed scope and the first unresolved implication

ORIGINAL is NOT_CLOSED. The unrestricted availability BARRIER and terminal
elimination remain unproved. The new result is a scoped whole-forest theorem,
not mere necessary identities, and not a complete cover of the old domain.

## Old domain
All actual minimal non-unimodal forests, without connectedness or a unique
large component. The inherited externally dependent component-30 audit forces
one >=31 component and permits at most two small components in 247 small
index cases. Large component shapes/counts and attached exteriors are unbounded.

## New excluded scope
Whole core-cover/private-arm forests satisfying PROOF.md (3.1). This includes
arbitrary core topology and arbitrary component count, with the explicit
all-parameter guarantee a>=192(q+1)^2 in the equal-arm case. Products of t
actually non-LC T(a,a,a) components are covered in the stated region. No LC
cofactor hypothesis is needed there: every component is retained in the
single core expansion.

The proof has no heavy computational dependency; its external mathematical
input is the known general decreasing-tail theorem. Four finite formula
controls verify the implementation, not the infinite scope. Global novelty
is not established. Not every arbitrary forest has the required private arms.

## New remaining domain
Uncoated arbitrary large trees; arbitrary completed exterior branches that
fail the cover or quantitative criterion; products of arbitrary non-LC large
components outside that criterion. A connected counterexample and multiple
large-component counterexamples remain possible within these unresolved
premises. No general bound on the remaining shape, size, or component count.

## First substantive missing arrow
The actual data of EVERY remaining forest must imply

    mu_k<=k+1 and k+1<beta
       => Var(T_k)<=mu_k(k+3-mu_k)+2 E[M_k],

or another plateau-safe mechanism excluding later rebounds. No universal
forest-specific estimate is supplied. Root-state mixture variance and size-
allocation variance are retained; checking them on five examples is not a
uniform bound. The exact expectation budget is a reformulation unless its
sign is controlled.

Alternatively, one would need a non-unimodality-preserving reduction from
all remaining minimum counterexamples to a proved class. Adding private arms
and proving the resulting graph unimodal is NOT such a reduction. The older
mandatory-terminal-structure -> smaller bad forest/contradiction arrow remains
unproved, as do J_H, RSM, and the universal prefix-LC condition.

The fixed search has 233 graphs, 1,240 nonvacuous middle BARRIER premises and
zero violations. Residual U/D pairs, real valleys and original counterexamples
remain zero. New HEREDITARY is UNKNOWN. No finite UNSAT or all-forest absence
claim, original Lean proof or independent peer review follows.
