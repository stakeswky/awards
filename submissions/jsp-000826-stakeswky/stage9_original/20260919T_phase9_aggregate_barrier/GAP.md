## General first unproved bridge
For independent k-sets in the whole forest, let mu,v,eta be the mean available
vertices, their variance, and mean edges among those vertices. Counting gives
mu_next=mu-1+(v-2eta)/mu. The sufficient plateau-safe claim still to prove is

    mu<=k+1 and k+1<beta  =>  v<=mu(k+3-mu)+2eta.

It would prevent the next ratio from exceeding one and, iterated with weak
inequalities, would forbid later rebounds including those after plateaus.
But no forest-specific proof of this bound is established. Reformulating
coefficient comparisons as moments is not itself progress on that universal
inequality. Component allocations add a second variance term, which has not
been uniformly bounded. The earlier fixed-union negative terms remain valid.

The compensation theorem uses A=prod s_ai being LC and being close to a
binomial Q in TOTAL coefficient mass. An arbitrary actual exterior can
invalidate those hypotheses. We have not proved that all remaining forests
satisfy them or can be reduced to graphs that do. The old mandatory-terminal
structure -> valid smaller bad forest/contradiction bridge also remains open.
