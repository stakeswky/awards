## What has actually been proved

C6-SC gives a full-support lower bound for the actual next coefficient decline:

 p_z-p_(z+1) >= [N_(z+1)(p_(z-1)-p_z)+C0+J-E]/N_z.

The proof includes the birth/death boundary, every inverse ratio cut, a proved
mass envelope for their TOTAL bill E, and an actual positive donor J. There is
also a fixed modal donor whose nonnegativity follows from component unimodality.
The denominator N_z is positive even when the current decline is zero.
The exact allocation budget R-V has the corresponding lower bound; no local
negative sigma or small supported allocation weight is discarded.

The implication "Payment>=0 implies no next rise" is proved. The statement
"Payment>=0 always holds in the remaining minimum-forest situation" is NOT.
This is a conditional signed comparison, not an unconditional payment theorem.

C6-LCFREE proves an infinite structural obstruction: for every r there is an
actual r-component forest for which every nonempty component subunion is non-LC.
With the expressly inherited Phase10 large-arm condition, even all those
subunions are unimodal. Thus component count or component-subunion unimodality
alone cannot justify a forced LC subunion. HEREDITARY is not asserted for this
construction, so the minimum-counterexample version is not refuted.

## Smallest remaining sufficient assertion

For a vertex-minimal bad disconnected forest F, choose a proper complete
component partition X,Y. Minimality supplies whole-unimodality of A and B.
At a residual history step in the mode band and the requested middle, prove
from actual forest/induced-subforest compatibility that

 E <= N_(z+1)(p_(z-1)-p_z)+C0+J,

with the quantities in PROOF Sections 3--6, or establish a different proved
entrance for every remaining candidate. A proof for one suitably justified
partition would suffice; merely selecting a partition in a finite test does not.
The exact R-V is independent of that selection and cannot improve by refinement.
No uniform positive lower bound for the weighted modal supply relative to E has
been obtained here. The modal weight may be zero; that cannot be silently ruled out.
