## 1. Actual objects, support, and the remaining target

Every graph in a forest statement here is finite, simple, undirected and unweighted.
For a forest F, p_i(F) counts its independent i-vertex sets. Put alpha=alpha(F).
The support is exactly 0,...,alpha because subsets of a maximum independent set
exist at every smaller size; p_0=1 and coefficients outside support are zero.
A non-unimodal sequence has a strict decrease followed later by a strict increase;
plateaus between them do not invalidate the test.

History(F,k) means there is i<=k with p_i>p_(i+1) and p_t>=p_(t+1) for i<=t<=k.
Define mu_k=(k+1)p_(k+1)/p_k, d_k=k+1-mu_k, and, for k<alpha,
e_k=mu_(k+1)-mu_k-1. The exact next-decrease budget is

    G_F(k)=mu_k(d_k-e_k)
          =(k+1)(k+2)(p_(k+1)-p_(k+2))/p_k.            (1)

No division by d_k is performed. Let M_max be the largest component order, not
an arm count. The inherited reporting range is

    h=floor(M_max(n-1)/(4M_max-2))+1,
    beta=ceil(alpha(n-1)/(n+alpha)),
    h<=k, k+1<beta.                                    (2)

The empty forest is trivial. The existing prefix and Basit--Galvin tail results
are used only to label the unresolved middle; the new local comparison does not
need them as hypotheses of its algebraic proof.

A genuine vertex-minimal bad forest has all smaller forests unimodal. Therefore
any nonempty proper complete component subunion and its complement are unimodal.
This does NOT give log-concavity, and does not make arbitrary large test graphs
HEREDITARY. In particular all ordinary grafted material graphs below have
HEREDITARY=UNKNOWN. No A or D existence conjecture is used.

## 3. Full signed convolution, with no LC entrance assumed

Let F=X disjoint-union Y be an actual complete component partition, with both
X,Y nonempty. Write A_i=p_i(X), B_j=p_j(Y), a=alpha(X)>=1 and b=alpha(Y)>=1.
Assume only that B is whole-unimodal. A need not be unimodal for the local
comparison, though minimality gives that too in the intended application.
Let m be the LAST modal index of B. Zero-pad A and B and set

    D_j=B_j-B_(j-1), 0<=j<=b+1;
    P_z=sum_(j<=m) D_j A_(z-j),
    N_z=sum_(t>m) (-D_t) A_(z-t).                       (3)

All sums here and below are finite. D_j>=0 for j<=m and D_t<=0 for t>m, with
D_0=B_0 and D_(b+1)=-B_b both included. Ordinary convolution, without deleting
any coefficient, gives

    p_z-p_(z-1)=P_z-N_z.                               (4)

For m+1<=z<=m+a put q=z-m-1. Then 0<=q<a. The last-mode convention gives
E_(m+1):=-D_(m+1)>0, including m=b, when this is the terminal death term.
Hence N_z>=E_(m+1) A_q>0 and N_(z+1)>=E_(m+1) A_(q+1)>0. This proves every
N denominator used below is positive, independently of any current plateau.

For z<=m, N_z=0; a strict decrease cannot occur there. For z>m+a, P_z=0
and remains zero at every larger index, so the convolution cannot rise later.
Thus the band m+1<=z<=m+a contains every possible unresolved descent-to-rise
step. It is not a coefficient truncation or an assertion that local sizes are
in a corresponding middle range.

## 4. Exact ratio-cut identity and all boundary terms

Define r_i=A_(i+1)/A_i for 0<=i<=a, including r_a=0, and

    c_l=r_l-r_(l+1), 0<=l<a.                           (5)

The sign of c_l is the sign of the LC minor at l+1, but we do NOT assume it
nonnegative. A negative c_l is an actual inverse ratio change of the component.
For each cut l define the actual nonnegative masses

    U_l=sum_(j<=m,z-j>l) D_j A_(z-j),
    V_l=sum_(t>m,0<=z-t<=l) (-D_t) A_(z-t),
    W_l=U_l V_l.                                      (6)

Also set C0=P_z A_0 max(0,-D_(z+1)). We claim the exact identity

    H_z:=P_z N_(z+1)-P_(z+1)N_z
        =C0+sum_(l=0)^(a-1) c_l W_l.                  (7)

Proof. Expand H_z as the sum over j<=m<t, with nonnegative multiplier
D_j(-D_t), of

    A_(z-j) A_(z+1-t)-A_(z+1-j) A_(z-t).              (8)

Put R=z-j and S=z-t. Then R>S, R>=q+1>=1, and S<=q<a. When 0<=S<R<=a,
(8) equals A_R A_S(r_S-r_R)=A_R A_S sum_(S<=l<R)c_l. Swapping finite sums
produces c_l times exactly U_l V_l. When S=-1, the sole surviving term is
A_R A_0; summing these t=z+1 terms gives C0. When S<-1, or R>a, the two
terms vanish. The case R=a is already covered by r_a=0 and introduces no
unsupported ratio beyond the positive support. These cases exhaust the
whole untruncated sum and prove (7). QED.

In particular W_q=P_z N_z. The individual Toeplitz terms (8) may be negative;
equation (7) does not discard them or replace their sum by an absolute variance.
Inverse ratio-cut contributions and negative local sigma budgets are different
objects. Both will be retained; neither is silently identified with the other.

## 5. A proved envelope for the TOTAL inverse-cut bill

Let

    H_l^A=max_(0<=i<=l) A_i,
    T_l^A=max_(l<i<=a) A_i.

Define

 Wbar_l=P_z min(N_z,H_l^A B_(z-l-1))       if l<q,
 Wbar_l=N_z min(P_z,T_l^A B_(z-l-1))       if l>=q.       (9)

Then 0<=W_l<=Wbar_l<=P_z N_z. This is an actual coefficient-mass bound,
not an assumption about LC allocation weights or negative association.

For l<q, all contributing positive D_j allocations have local index z-j>l,
so U_l=P_z. Every V_l term has t>=z-l>m and A_(z-t)<=H_l^A. Include the
entire remaining decreasing tail of B, including its death at b+1:

    V_l<=H_l^A sum_(t>=z-l)(B_(t-1)-B_t)
        =H_l^A B_(z-l-1).

Together with V_l<=N_z this gives the first case of (9). If z-l>b+1,
both the relevant sum and the padded B coefficient are zero.

For l>=q, all nonzero negative-D allocations have z-t<=q<=l, so V_l=N_z.
Every U_l term has j<=z-l-1<=m and A_(z-j)<=T_l^A. Include the entire
increasing prefix of B, starting with its birth D_0:

    U_l<=T_l^A sum_(j<=z-l-1)(B_j-B_(j-1))
        =T_l^A B_(z-l-1).

Together with U_l<=P_z this proves the second case. If z-l-1<0 the sum is
empty and the padded coefficient is zero. No negative sigma term or small
probability is rounded to zero in this argument. QED.

Choose just ONE available positive cut:

    J_z=max({0} union {c_l W_l:c_l>0}),
    E_z=sum_(c_l<0) (-c_l) Wbar_l,
    L_z=C0+J_z-E_z.                                   (10)

Equation (7), the nonnegativity of every other positive cut, and (9) prove

    H_z>=L_z.                                         (11)

Thus the entire inverse bill is bounded by the explicit E_z. A donor is a
real positive summand of the exact expansion, not an invented supply or an
object-count matching with an unproved capacity. Only one donor is credited;
we do not re-spend it across separate negative bills. All inverse cuts enter
the same total E_z. The max can be replaced by any one explicitly chosen
positive cut, at the price of a weaker valid lower bound.

### A donor supplied by component unimodality, not by a new LC assumption

If A is itself unimodal, let u be its last modal index. For a nonempty forest
u>=1. The modal cut has

    c_(u-1)=(A_u^2-A_(u-1) A_(u+1))/(A_(u-1) A_u)>=0.

This sign follows merely because A_u is at least both neighbors (the next
coefficient is zero if u=a). Consequently one may FIX the donor to
J_modal=c_(u-1) W_(u-1), rather than choose the largest positive cut in (10).
Every argument below remains valid with this smaller donor. In a minimum bad
forest the needed A-unimodality comes from minimality of the proper block X.
This identifies a positive structural source that is actually supplied by the
common hypothesis. It does not show that its weighted magnitude is large
enough: W_(u-1) can vanish, and the entire inverse bill still must be paid.
The material audit checks the fixed-modal version separately; it passes all
ten qualified middle positions. This finite result is not an automatic-entrance
proof for arbitrary forests.

## 6. C6-SC-v1: real decline pays the bill, with no division by d

Combining (4) and (7) gives

 N_z(p_z-p_(z+1))
   =N_(z+1)(p_(z-1)-p_z)+H_z
   >=N_(z+1)(p_(z-1)-p_z)+L_z.                        (12)

Define the right side as Payment_z. Since N_z>0,

    p_z-p_(z+1)>=Payment_z/N_z.                       (13)

This proves the claimed numerical structural gate: **Payment_z>=0 forbids
the next increase**. The previous/current real decline in (12) has a proved
coefficient multiplier N_(z+1); it is not merely a nonnegative number asserted
to be available capacity. The boundary C0 and the actual positive cut J_z are
additional proved supplies, and the full inverse envelope E_z is the bill.

A plateau p_(z-1)=p_z contributes zero decline supply, but (13) remains valid.
No division by the plateau value or by d_k occurs. A negative Payment_z means
only this sufficient estimate failed; it does not prove the next step rises.
Indeed (11) may be strict because most positive cuts are deliberately not used.

For k=z-1 and any actual complete-component partition, (1) and (13) imply

    R-V=G_F(k)
       >=(k+1)(k+2) Payment_z/[p_k(F)N_z].             (14)

A true History(F,k) and nonnegative Payment_z therefore give the desired
no-rebound inequality e_k<=d_k, including the reporting middle (2). There is
no full-LC-window hypothesis. The local comparison allows arbitrary numbers,
locations and signs of c_l, provided the explicit total payment is nonnegative.

If the same F=X disjoint-union Y passes this gate at every history step in the
mode band, (4) outside the band and (13) inside it prove complete unimodality.
This is a conditional all-step theorem, NOT a proof that the gates are automatic.
For more than two components, X and Y may themselves be any nonempty complete
subunions; the same formula applies without asserting a history for either block.
In the minimum-counterexample application, B-unimodality follows from smaller
order, not from an unproved arbitrary-unimodal convolution rule.

**Unproved arrow.** We have not shown that actual forest compatibility plus
HEREDITARY forces Payment_z>=0 at every residual step, or forces another already
proved entrance. Consequently C6-SC is a proved conditional comparison, not the
unrestricted C4-F theorem. Replacing the exact deficit by an existential choice
of partition cannot remove this missing arrow.

## 7. Reconcile (14) with all signed component allocations

For a uniform independent k-set in a forest Z, let the original-graph residual
be Z-N[S], with T remaining vertices and c remaining components. Put
sigma_Z=4mu_Z-Var(T)-2E[c]. Double counting available independent pairs and
using |E(Z-N[S])|=T-c gives

 sigma_Z(j)=mu_Z(j)+mu_Z(j)^2-(j+1)(j+2)p_(j+2)(Z)/p_j(Z).

At j=alpha(Z), T=c=mu=sigma=0. No zero-denominator extension is needed.
For F=X disjoint-union Y, every supported allocation i has

    w_i=A_i B_(k-i)/p_k(F),
    M_i=mu_X(i)+mu_Y(k-i).

Conditional independence of the two selected sets and total variance give

 R=sum_i w_i(sigma_X(i)+sigma_Y(k-i))+mu_F d_F,
 V=Var_w(M_i),   R-V=G_F(k).                          (15)

The sums range from max(0,k-alpha(Y)) to min(k,alpha(X)), with all endpoints
and all negative local sigmas. Formula (14) proves a lower bound for THIS
signed difference through a separate coefficient comparison. It does not
replace these weights by D_j weights or delete allocations from (15).

Refining a component partition increases R and V by the same conditional
variance. Their difference is unchanged. This inherited identity explains
why changing partitions by itself is not progress; the new gate instead
specifies an actual total coefficient payment.

## 8. C6-LCFREE-v1: arbitrarily many components with no LC subunion

For each integer s>=1 construct the actual tree T_s with a central root,
three adjacent hubs, and s private two-edge paths at each hub. All new
vertices are distinct and there are no other edges. It has 6s+4 vertices.
Partition independent sets by the central root and hub choices to obtain

 A_s(x)=[(1+2x)^s+x(1+x)^s]^3+x(1+2x)^(3s).           (16)

This is a complete graph-count formula, not a weighted substitute. Write
u=2^s+s, v=s*2^(s-1)+binom(s,2) and d=3s+3. The degree-s+1 hub factor
has last coefficients v,u,1. Therefore the last three coefficients of (16)
are exactly

    (3u^2+3v+2^(3s), 3u, 1).                         (17)

The root-selected term ends at d-2, hence its 2^(3s) contribution in (17).
For t disjoint copies, multiplication of the last three coefficients gives

 [x^(td)] A_s^t=1,
 [x^(td-1)] A_s^t=3tu,
 [x^(td-2)] A_s^t=t(3u^2+3v+2^(3s))+binom(t,2)9u^2.

The penultimate LC minor is thus

    t[((9t+3)/2)u^2-3v-2^(3s)].                      (18)

THEOREM. For every integer r>=1 and every integer s>=6 satisfying 2^s>24r,
every nonempty complete component subunion of F=disjoint union of r copies
of T_s has a non-LC independence polynomial.

Proof. Such a subunion is t copies for some 1<=t<=r. Since s<=2^s,
u<=2^(s+1), and

 ((9t+3)/2)u^2 <= (18t+6)2^(2s)
                    <=24r*2^(2s)<2^(3s).

Subtracting the positive 3v makes (18) strictly negative. QED.

This is nonempty for every r; s=6r is one explicit choice: 64>24 at r=1, and each increment of r
multiplies 2^(6r) by 64 but multiplies 24r by at most 2.
Consequently there is NO absolute number of components that guarantees a
nonempty LC complete component subunion in every forest. Taking more components
or enlarging a fixed palette does not establish such an entrance.

This is NOT an original counterexample and does not assert HEREDITARY. It
also does not refute a statement with an additional ACTUAL-minimum-bad premise.
That premise must provide genuine extra information rather than be ignored.
A stronger caution is available using, explicitly, the old Phase-10 Corollary
7.2: choose s>=192(4r+1)^2 as well. For every 1<=t<=r the inherited sufficient
theorem makes T_s^t unimodal, while (18) still makes every such subunion non-LC.
That all-subunion unimodality is an inherited result, not a new C6 arm threshold,
and still is not a proof of HEREDITARY for all induced subforests.

  There is no earlier strict descent and this is not a qualified middle case.

The distinction between a negative ratio cut, a negative Toeplitz pair and a
negative local sigma is explicit in the certificates. In particular a negative
ratio-cut term need not make a full Toeplitz determinant negative: intervening
positive curvature can cancel it. No class is claimed to have all three phenomena
at every tested position.

## 10. Attempt at the remaining entrance and exact stopping boundary

The directed graft lane preserves total size, current index, full forest edges,
whole-sequence scanning and the non-LC nature of both components. It optimizes
the actual inverse-bill / proved-supply ratio, not a small current d_k. Among
250 labelled proposals, 97 nontrivial proposals met the relevant gates/history
for a cut test. None violated the payment gate and no full original valley was
found. These are proposal evaluations, not 97 nonisomorphic new forests. The
material best graph received independent full recounts. Ordinary intermediate
proposals did not all receive a second graph-count algorithm.

Neither this finite result nor the displayed inequalities establish an automatic
entrance for all remaining shapes. No valid counterexample to C6-SC was found;
no actual forest refutation of its automatic-gate strengthening was found in
the completed lane. That strengthening remains UNPROVED, not proved by these tests.

The abstract-array audit illustrates why unimodality alone is insufficient:
there are positive unimodal A,B for which A*B has a genuine valley. Such arrays
violate forest low-order/degree constraints and are labelled NONFOREST. They
cannot refute ORIGINAL or the minimum-forest entrance. They do show that replacing
forest compatibility by arbitrary unimodal arrays loses an essential premise.

The first unproved step is to bound the complete inverse bill by the proved
supply uniformly for every residual minimum bad forest, or force a different
already proved entrance using minimality. The LC-free theorem prevents the
unconditional component-count shortcut, but does not settle the minimality-
restricted version. General C4-F and ORIGINAL therefore remain NOT_CLOSED.
