# A7 to D: a same-leaf history interface, not ADMC

For E arbitrary rooted tree and a bare pendant path of N>=128(alpha(E)+2)^2
new vertices, put F_m=E plus its first m path vertices. On the actual tip
edge l=xN,w=x_(N-1), C=I(F_(N-2)), H=I(F_(N-3)). Both states of E remain
in every F_m. Section6 proves the quantitative ratio shortening for m=N-2..N,
not a generic common-factor peak-preservation assertion.

Actual current decline in T forces C_(k+1)<C_k, H_(k+1)<H_k and the local
factorial minor needed in the exact leaf factorization. Consequently both
original-graph tip budgets are positive. The full first-fall history also
contracts as R_(k+1)<=(1-5/N)^(k+1-i)R_i. No valley premise is attached to an
arbitrary merely bad ADMC witness; ADMC itself is NOT proved by these statements.

The A5 same-edge factorization and full distant positive sum are retained,
not canceled or counted as an extra donor. This scoped rooted count interface
is offered for use only with its long-path graph gate. We have not proved
that a general minimum bad graph can be transformed into this gate. There is
no assumption about a paying root/leaf in C/D's unproved general cases.
