# Exact target, scope and findings

## Original problem

F is a finite simple undirected unweighted forest, possibly disconnected.
p_k counts independent k-subsets of V(F), including p_0=1. Let n=|V(F)| and
alpha=max{k:p_k>0}. All coefficients on 0,...,alpha are positive; append zeros
to alpha+1,...,n. ORIGINAL asks for a peak m with

    p_0 <= ... <= p_m >= ... >= p_n.

A legal refutation requires an actual validated forest and indices i<j such
that p_i>p_(i+1) and p_j<p_(j+1). Looking only for a neighboring strict valley
would miss a plateau-spanning rebound; both the main sign scan and independent
peak-definition audit use the complete sequence.

No such forest was found in this run. ORIGINAL remains NOT_CLOSED.

## Separate directed targets

Use beta=ceil(alpha(n-1)/(n+alpha)). The Basit-Galvin tail theorem and its scope
are inherited from the pinned Phase-8/9/10 records. It is not a new result here.
The tests are:

* PREFIX_BETA: L_k=p_k^2-p_(k-1)p_(k+1)>=0 for every 1<=k<beta.
* Plateau-safe BARRIER: for 0<=k<beta-1, p_(k+1)<=p_k implies
  p_(k+2)<=p_(k+1).
* ORIGINAL: no strict negative difference followed later by a positive one.

The first two are sufficient-route checks, not interchangeable definitions of
ORIGINAL. In particular a weak-descent BARRIER failure beginning with equality
is not by itself a legal ORIGINAL counterexample. The regression sequence
(1,2,2,3,1), used only as a synthetic test and NOT as a forest, checks that
separation. Non-LC alone also never counts as an ORIGINAL failure.

The selected-count grouped-range sufficient estimate was already refuted in
Phase 12, while the exact condition was not. This run neither treats that
substitute as true nor repackages its old auxiliary counterexamples as success.
No new Window-A proposition was visible at the last recovered head/comments;
there is no claim to have tested an unseen or uncommitted A statement.

## Actual finite construction ranges

Irregular structures: 96 partial decorations on eight nonuniform branching
cores, 96 mixed-depth root amalgams, and 12 deep branching joins. After exact
unrooted isomorphism deduplication this lane has 201 trees. Arms have lengths
1 through 5; many core edges retain uncoated endpoints. Only specified root
roles are used, not all root-isomorphism classes.

Exterior grafts: six unequal-arm bush seeds, eight branching external trees,
three selected attachment roles, and zero or two intervening vertices give
288 actual complete trees. The external vertices and all their edges remain
in both counting algorithms. No exterior polynomial is dropped or cancelled.
These constructions do not assert that no other theorem could cover a member;
only the earlier private-arm or fixed alternating-path theorem is not used to
exclude or count them automatically.

Products: 23 verified connected non-LC bank trees of order >30, all 253 distinct
pairs, and 24 specified combinations each of sizes 3,4,5,6 were proposed.
Three quintuples and seven sextuples exceeded 2400 vertices and were excluded;
339 products were completely counted. The bank includes unequal-depth and
uncovered-exterior shapes, not just repetitions of one T(a,a,a) component.

Fixed-order relocation: six trajectories each propose 120 remove/reconnect
moves on real tree edges. Three optimize prefix-LC proximity and three a
full-sequence falling-then-rising margin. Each trajectory keeps its vertex
count fixed. Floats rank moves only; all decisions about mathematical failure
use arbitrary-precision integer comparisons. Including initial copies gives
726 proposals; eight duplicates leave 718 additional exact graphs.

All generators and selected roles are deterministic from seed 99320260920.
Their ranges are explicit finite search surfaces, not all-order structural
classification. AHU deduplication is global inside this run only.

## Findings and non-findings

The 1546 accepted forests contain 464 non-LC examples. All 339 heterogeneous
products are non-LC and unimodal. First non-LC indices in this corpus are at
least beta+13. For the closest prefix ratio, the actual forest has n=2343,
alpha=1401, beta=877, k=700 and

    p_(k-1)*p_(k+1)/p_k^2 = approximately 0.9967571698307909 < 1.

The complete integers and strictly positive 1054-digit minor are recorded in
AUDIT.json and regenerated by source. The decimal above is descriptive only.
A ratio close to one is not an original valley and can improve simply with
order, which is why the relocation trajectories keep order fixed.

All 222267 tested prefix positions passed. All 28032 nonvacuous BARRIER
positions passed, but zero had an equality premise. Six fully checked A/B
profiles have U=D and zero residual pairs. No HEREDITARY certification,
minimal counterexample or nonvacuous RSM test is claimed.

No true or directed-claim failure candidate triggered reduction. Therefore
original candidate reductions = 0; inventing a reduction of a non-LC graph as
an original-failure candidate would be invalid. The absence of failure is
finite evidence, not a proof that an auxiliary statement is true.

## Universal gap

This run proves no new general structural theorem. Arbitrarily large trees,
arbitrary exterior attachments, and arbitrary collections of non-LC components
are still unresolved beyond existing scoped results. A theorem that all tested
products pass does not justify closure under arbitrary unimodal convolution.
The core original problem remains NOT_CLOSED.
