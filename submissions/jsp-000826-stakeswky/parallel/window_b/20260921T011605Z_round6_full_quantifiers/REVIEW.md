# B6 internal review

Review type: same-model mathematical review plus separate exact graph,
pair-enumeration and flow algorithms. Not external peer review.

1. Batch identity: the mounted B5-172708Z archive and actual early PR comment
   for B5-172930Z are distinct. Only the former supplied reused source/input
   bytes. The latter's complete source/archive was not obtained or rerun.
   Neither batch's counts are reported as fresh B6 coverage.
2. Graph counters: forest_exact.py and independent_check.py are unchanged.
   Graph validation excludes cycles, loops and duplicate edges. Every graph
   edit recomputes n,M,alpha,h,beta; alpha was separately checked. No weighted
   or coefficient-only surrogate is accepted as an original graph.
3. Quantifiers: every leaf and all roots at the SAME applicable k are kept in
   the primary profiles. Full mode intervals and actual history are recorded.
   LOCAL is required before U,D are defined; it is never called HEREDITARY.
   Best material profiles receive ALL A/B independent recounts, whereas the
   ordinary primary profiles do not claim all-array dual verification.
4. Original predicate: the full sequence sign scan and peak-definition test
   include platforms and zero padding. Negative local budgets, an ADMC
   distance1, or a single bad root do not count as a theorem refutation.
5. Network completeness: two 16-vertex instances enumerate every nonempty
   ordered-pair group, every positive target (including zero remaining
   capacity), and every permitted ONE arc. Empty zero/zero groups have no
   objects or capacities and are omitted. Independent literal pair enumeration
   agrees with the D-orientation/exterior generation for EVERY group.
6. Reservations: source overlaps are charged once. E4, Phase13 at j4 only,
   and the new E5-JC cases have explicit outgoing and full inverse ledgers.
   Early E5-ONE and B6's final-JC composite are separate versions. Their
   reservations happen to coincide on the two new full-network instances.
7. Flow arithmetic: sparse capacities and indices are explicitly bounded
   within signed integer limits; graph coefficients remain arbitrary-precision.
   Dinic and Edmonds-Karp agree. Actual assignments are checked independently.
   64 small software networks additionally agree with exhaustive Hall subsets.
   No solver success flag alone certifies a matching or a cut.
8. New one-vertex theorem: the complete1575-source core table has distinct
   chosen targets of zero-high/two-unit signature. All known target loads
   are zero. The proof exhausts core and exterior inverse centers and fixes
   z across all C0, avoiding cross-fiber collisions. Already paid E5 sources
   are omitted. The theorem requires a complete T26 component and does not
   replace an arbitrary symmetric-difference block by one.
9. HEREDITARY: each new16-vertex material graph has all65535 proper induced
   subsets dual-checked. T26 uses a complete connected-induced-component
   classification (2816 root parameter records and15 LC nonroot types), not
   a claim of 2^26 separate DP calls. LC convolution supplies the exterior.
10. Repairs: the first dense NetworkX full-network attempt exhausted memory,
    recorded by cgroup oom_kill, and produced no completed network verdict.
    Its output is excluded. Sparse integer flow changes storage, not the
    network or graph counters. A summary-only key mismatch (delta_next versus
    next_delta) was repaired, with failed log/receipt retained. It did not
    change any graph or integer count. The final full source replay reran all
    searches and checks successfully; incomplete attempts are never added.
11. Documentation: group counts mean groups containing at least one relevant
    ordered pair; zero/zero empty groups are excluded. The explanatory text
    clarifies this without changing the mathematical objects or permits.
12. Publication: only the new B6 subtree may be appended. Use a freshly read
    parent and non-force fast-forward, retain concurrent D6/other work, and
    reread actual contents/hashes plus PR Draft status. No CI result settles
    ORIGINAL. Final actual publication IDs belong to its separate receipt.
